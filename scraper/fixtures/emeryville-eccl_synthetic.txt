Emeryville Center of Community Life — Aquatics
Recreation Swim Schedule

The ECCL outdoor pool is open for Recreation Swim during the summer season.

Season: June 22 – August 14, 2026
Monday – Thursday: 1:00 pm – 3:00 pm
Friday – Sunday: 2:00 pm – 4:00 pm

Extended Fall Season: August 15 – September 27, 2026
Saturday – Sunday only: 2:00 pm – 4:00 pm

Admission:
  Adults (18+): $6.00 drop-in
  Youth (5–17): $4.00 drop-in
  Children under 4: Free

Pool Rules:
- All children under 8 must be accompanied by an adult in the water.
- No flotation devices except Coast Guard approved life jackets.
- Lap Swim is not available during Recreation Swim hours.

Lap Swim (Adults only):
Monday – Friday: 6:00 am – 8:00 am
Saturday: 8:00 am – 10:00 am

Swim Lessons:
Monday – Friday: 9:00 am – 12:00 pm (various levels, registration required)
